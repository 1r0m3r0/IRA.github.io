Abstract

We investigated the effect of Machine Learning-driven personalized learning paths on student performance in online STEM courses. A randomized controlled trial (n=342) compared AI-personalized vs. traditional fixed curriculum over one semester. Results show a 23% improvement in final grades (p<0.01) and 18% higher retention. Methodology

Participants were randomly assigned to control (n=171) and experimental (n=171) groups. The AI system used a deep reinforcement learning model to adapt content difficulty. Learning outcomes were measured through standardized tests. Data was analyzed using mixed-effects models controlling for demographics, prior GPA, and course discipline.
